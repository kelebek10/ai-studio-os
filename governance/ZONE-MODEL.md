## 1. Purpose

This document defines the approved Zone Model for PEYZAJ AI / PAI-FORGE. It defines how geographic and ecological operating zones are identified, related, governed and versioned without making zone identifiers carry semantic meaning.

## 2. Approved Rules

### ZM-01 — Variable depth

- Province (`il`) and district (`ilçe`) are mandatory geographic levels.
- Lower levels may be introduced where ecological or microclimate resolution requires them.
- The hierarchy must remain extensible and must not encode fixed depth assumptions into the identifier.

### ZM-02 — Zone identity

- `zone_id` is `BIGINT GENERATED BY DEFAULT AS IDENTITY PRIMARY KEY`.
- `zone_id` carries no semantic hierarchy or geographic meaning.

### ZM-03 — Parent relationship

- `parent_zone_id` is a separate nullable foreign key to `zone(zone_id)`.
- `display_path` is derived/cache/read-only presentation data and is never authoritative identity.

### ZM-04 — Geographic parent vs scientific fallback

- Geographic hierarchy and scientific fallback are separate concepts.
- A fallback relationship must never be inferred from geographic parentage.
- Scientific fallback rules live in a separate human-approved governance structure.

### ZM-05 — Microclimate separation

Microclimate is a profile of a zone, not a replacement for zone identity or boundary. Initial controlled criteria are:

- `deniz_etkisi`: `KIYI | YAKIN | UZAK`
- `ruzgar_maruziyeti`: `DUSUK | ORTA | YUKSEK`
- `tuzluluk_riski`: `VAR | YOK`

The model is extensible without changing the meaning of `zone_id`.

### ZM-06 — Human authority

The Human Project Owner is the final authority for zone creation, merge, split, fallback and material hierarchy decisions.

Material zone decisions require:

- `reason_category`
- `reason`
- `evidence_reference`

AI or automated research may propose changes but may not make authoritative zone-state transitions.

### ZM-07 — Lifecycle

Zone lifecycle supports controlled add, merge, split and passive states.

- No destructive delete of an authoritative zone identity.
- Merge does not equal delete.
- Split does not imply automatic knowledge migration.
- Existing Knowledge/Evidence relationships require explicit controlled handling after merge/split.
- Automatic migration of authoritative scientific data is prohibited unless separately approved.

### ZM-08 — Authoritative dictionary

PostgreSQL is the authoritative Zone Dictionary.

Google Sheets is a candidate/research interface only and cannot create authoritative `zone_id` values or modify the production Zone Dictionary directly.

### ZM-09 — Candidate reference

`candidate_ref` is provenance/reference metadata, not identity.

Repeated submission of the same candidate must not automatically create a new `zone_id`. Production identity assignment is controlled by the Zone Dictionary workflow.

### ZM-10 — Deterministic identity protection

Candidate/source identity matching must use deterministic, reviewable rules. String similarity or AI inference must not silently create, merge or replace authoritative zone identity.

## 3. Governance Separation

The following distinctions are mandatory:

```text
ZONE IDENTITY        ≠ ZONE NAME
ZONE IDENTITY        ≠ DISPLAY PATH
ZONE IDENTITY        ≠ CANDIDATE REF
ZONE                 ≠ MICROCLIMATE PROFILE
ZONE                 ≠ SCIENTIFIC FALLBACK RULE
GEOGRAPHIC PARENT    ≠ SCIENTIFIC FALLBACK
AI PROPOSAL          ≠ AUTHORITATIVE ZONE STATE
```

## 4. Relationship to Knowledge

Zone is a reusable geographic/scientific context entity. Knowledge records reference zones through the normalized `record_zone` relationship.

The Knowledge record remains authoritative for its own `kapsam` semantics. Zone relationships do not redefine Knowledge scope.

The approved scope rules are:

- `ZONA_OZEL` → exactly one zone.
- `BOLGESEL` → one or more zones.
- `GENEL` → no zone relationship.
- A Knowledge record must not mix `ZONA_OZEL` and `BOLGESEL_UYE` relations.

These cross-table rules are to be transactionally enforced when `knowledge_record` and `record_zone` are finalized.

## 5. Approval and Audit

Zone changes are recorded as immutable approval/history events. Current state and historical event history are separate concerns.

Concurrency, idempotency and event ordering are mandatory controls:

- `expected_version` must match current state before an approval transition.
- Successful approval increments the current version exactly once.
- `event_sequence` provides unique ordering; gaps are permitted.
- `idempotency_key` prevents duplicate approval processing.
